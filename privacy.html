Privacy Policy

How Truvia Consultants collects, uses, and protects the personal information of our clients and website visitors.

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Truvia Consultants ("we", "us", or "our") is an immigration consultancy based in Salmiya, Kuwait. We are committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who contacts us or uses our website at truviaconsultants.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide to us directly, including when you complete our consultation or assessment forms, email us, or contact us via phone or WhatsApp. This may include:

  • Your name and contact details (phone/WhatsApp number, email address)
  • Your occupation and preferred destination country
  • Details about your migration goals and any information you choose to share in your message
  • Documents and personal details you provide during a consultation or case assessment (such as qualifications, work history, and identification, where relevant to your application)

We also automatically collect limited technical information when you visit our website, such as your browser type, device, and pages viewed, through analytics tools (see Section 3).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide the consultancy services you request
  • Assess your eligibility for migration pathways and prepare applications on your behalf
  • Communicate with you about your case, our services, and relevant updates
  • Improve our website and understand how visitors use it
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors interact with our pages. Google Analytics may set cookies to collect anonymous usage data such as page views and session duration. This information is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate our website and deliver our services. When you submit a form on our website, your details are processed through Web3Forms in order to deliver your enquiry to us by email. We also use Google Analytics as described above. These providers process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain your case records, and meet our legal and professional obligations. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymise it.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or update it, and to request that we delete it where we are not required to retain it. To make any such request, please contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, please contact us:
